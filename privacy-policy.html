Privacy Policy

Sudoku Master

Last updated: 27/9/2025

Information We Collect

Sudoku Master is designed to respect your privacy. We collect minimal information to provide you with the best gaming experience:

  • Game statistics (games played, completion times, difficulty preferences)
  • App settings and preferences
  • Local game progress and saved games

All this information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Track your game progress and statistics
  • Maintain your app preferences and settings
  • Provide personalized daily challenges
  • Improve your overall gaming experience

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using secure storage methods. We do not:

  • Send your data to external servers
  • Share your information with third parties
  • Require account creation or personal information
  • Track your location or device information

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Local data encryption
  • Secure storage practices
  • No network transmission of personal data
  • Regular security updates

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Reset game statistics at any time
  • Clear all app data through device settings
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data
  • No account deletion needed as no accounts are created

Third-Party Services

Sudoku Master does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or social media services. The app functions entirely offline without external dependencies.

Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for users of all ages. We do not collect personal information from children or adults, making it completely safe for family use.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and indicated by the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please feel free to contact us through:

  • App store reviews
  • In-app feedback options

We are committed to maintaining your privacy and will respond to any concerns promptly.
